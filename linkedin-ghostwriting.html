FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you make posts sound like me?

Every engagement starts with a voice capture session: a structured conversation where I learn how you think, what you believe, and how you naturally express ideas. I also review any existing content you have written. By the time I write your first post, I have a clear picture of your tone, your opinions, and the language you actually use. Most clients say it sounds more like them than anything they would have written themselves.

How many posts per month?

Most clients opt for 8 to 12 posts per month, which hits the algorithm sweet spot of two to three posts per week. We can scale up or down depending on your goals and budget. I will always recommend a cadence that is sustainable and consistent rather than a burst that goes quiet.

Do I need to review everything before it goes out?

Yes, always. I deliver a batch of posts for your review at the start of each period. You approve, adjust, or send back for revisions. Nothing goes live without your sign-off. Some clients make very few changes. Others use the review cycle to add personal anecdotes or timely references. Either approach works.

Will people know you wrote it?

Ghostwriting is a long-established professional practice. The ideas, opinions and expertise in every post come from you. I shape and structure them. Executives in every sector work this way. What matters to your audience is whether the content is credible, valuable and consistent.

What if I don't think I have anything interesting to say?

You do. You are operating at the sharp end of one of the most dynamic sectors in the world. Regulatory shifts, product decisions, customer behaviour, market trends. Your everyday context is genuinely interesting to the people you are trying to reach. My job is to help you see the story in what you already know.
