Context

Axiomledger has a strong product but an untested story. The technology is genuinely differentiated: independent, continuous proof that digital entitlements match real underlying assets, anchored so records can't be altered. Goldlagerhaus proves it works.

Two years in, the challenge now is finding an audience, and the message hasn't been tested at scale. The goal is to become known enough that the right companies come to you.

Approach

Our initial conversation focused on LinkedIn as a place to start building a marketing process, but to provide long term value, I think we need to integrate some foundational planning at the start, and then use what we learn from content creation to create the most compelling version of the AxiomLedger story we can.

Here's what that looks like:

Process

1

Messaging framework

Define the core story: what this is, who it's for, key use cases and measurable value. The backbone for everything else.

2

Content plan

A posting plan mapped to your target sectors and partners, so every piece pulls in a commercial direction.

3

Article sequence

Founder-bylined pieces, built from short interviews and written in your voice. With practical guidance on promotion, since content needs activating from a standing start.

4

Website

Once the message is proven, it gets integrated into a new version of your site as the credible backstop for every lead the content brings.

Structure

The work runs as one ongoing engagement, roughly a day a week, with a heavier first month to lay the foundation.

Month 1: Foundation

The messaging framework, content plan, and first block of articles. Front-loaded, because getting the message right is where the value starts.

Month 2 onward: Cadence

A steady rhythm of published, promoted content, with the message refined as it meets its audience. Roughly a day a week. Output 1–2 articles, depending on if we're working on anything else.

Website: separate project

Quoted separately once the message is proven, so your site is built on something tested.

Why me

Fintech and trust fluency. Ten years writing for regulated and complex fintech: lending, payments, banking infrastructure, DeFi. I understand your technology and the conservative industries you sell into.

Claim discipline. Used to working in regulated, compliance-led industries, where claims are scrutinised and hyperbole loses leads.

Strategic mindset. I've run acquisition and conversion as a marketing manager, and run fractional content for fintechs today. I think about results, not words.

What I'd need from you

Interview time early on to extract your vision, then a short interview each week to capture your thinking, plus quick sign-off on drafts. The design goal is to make that the lightest possible ask while producing content that carries your expertise.
